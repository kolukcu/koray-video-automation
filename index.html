Koray Video Automation

A private publishing tool for a small group of independent YouTube channels.

What this app does

Koray Video Automation is an internal tool run by Koray Olukcu, an independent creator in the Netherlands. It helps produce and publish explainer videos to channels that he owns and manages. It is not offered to the public, and no outside users can sign in.

The app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload finished videos to the owner's own channels, always as private first;
  • set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and make a video public or schedule it only after the owner has reviewed and approved it;
  • read view, watch-time and audience statistics of the owner's own channels to see which videos work best.
