Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Koray Video Automation (“the app”, “we”) is a private tool operated by Koray Olukcu in the Netherlands. It is used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels: The Buyout Brief, Checkout Line and Ground Truth.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects one of his channels through Google sign-in, the app accesses only that channel's data that is needed for its tasks:

  • basic channel information (channel name and ID);
  • the channel's videos, playlists and their settings, so it can upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy status;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (for example views, watch time, traffic sources and audience countries).

The app does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users beyond the aggregated statistics and public comments that YouTube shows to the channel owner.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to analyse how his own channels perform. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, except with the service providers that run the app's automation (such as the workflow host n8n) and only to perform these tasks.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted in the app's workflow tool. Statistics may be kept in private tables for up to 30 days before they are refreshed from YouTube, or longer as summary figures (for example the total views of a video) for the owner's own reports. Nothing is published on this website.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked, stored data from the YouTube API Services is deleted within 7 days.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in its own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the About page of the channels listed on the home page.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
